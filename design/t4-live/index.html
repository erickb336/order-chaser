Live trading (T4): clickable prototype

This prototype shows how the order chaser sends real orders to Kraken. It follows direction B of the dry run (status card and event log) and the margin screens of T5. Every number, id and date is sample data: there is no real key and no real order.

Status: approved, the version to build. It shows the owner's answers Q1 to Q10 and the PE's changes C1 to C9. See the Decisions page for each one and its state link. The build (T4) starts after T5 merges.

The flow

1. Setup: account and key→ 2. Switch to live→ 3. Live confirm→ 4. Live chase→ 5. Result
After a crash:6. Restart reconcile→Result

Rules the screens keep

  • The key never shows on a page after it is saved, not even a part of it.
  • Permission names are the labels of the Kraken support article: Query Funds, Modify Orders, Cancel/Close Orders, Query Open Orders & Trades, Query Closed Orders & Trades, Access WebSockets API, Withdraw Funds. Setup asks you to check them against your Kraken key page.
  • After the safety timer fires, the page lists the other orders that Kraken cancelled, stop-loss and take-profit orders first.
  • Setup shows the real host and port (127.0.0.1:5180 in the sample).
  • The fill percent rounds down. It shows 100% only when all of the amount filled.
  • When the tool ignores Fill the rest now or Stop, the page says so and gives the reason.
  • Chase names show seconds (Buy 0.0500 BTC · 09:52:04).
  • Dark by default. No text uses an opacity fade.

Not in this prototype: the history list (unchanged from T2 and T5), the light theme, and the first live test itself (it needs your go-ahead at that time).
