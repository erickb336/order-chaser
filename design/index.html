Order chaser: screen design for your approval

This is a clickable prototype of the browser page for the Kraken Pro order chaser (task T2). It comes after the research (T1), where you chose: a page on localhost:5180, Kraken Pro only, the start ask as the cap with a per-trade override, and an IOC at the start ask after the timeout. Nothing is built yet. All numbers are sample data, and no page calls Kraken.

What I need from you:
  1. Pick a direction for the live chase screen. I recommend B, the status card. Compare the 3 directions.
  2. Answer the open questions, or accept the defaults. See the 14 questions.
  3. Approve the flow below, or mark what to change.

1. The core screen: 3 directions

compare.html

Live chase: price ladder vs status card vs timeline

Same chase, same state, three shapes, side by side. Switch the state to compare. Strength, weakness and the recommendation are on the page.

2. The full flow, in direction B

1 · setup.html

First-run setup

Local tool check, API key and its permissions, the safety timer warning. 6 states.

 2 · new.html

New chase

Pair, side, amount, dry run or live, cap and override, timeout. 11 states.

 3 · chase.html

Live chase

The status card, the price rail and the event log. 12 states, live and dry run.

 4 · result.html

Result

Filled, average price, fees, maker vs taker, saving against a market order at the start. 5 outcomes.

 5 · history.html

History

Past chases with outcome and saving. List, empty, loading, failure.

 questions.html

Questions for the owner

14 open product cases, each with options, my recommendation and a default.

3. Every state of the live chase

Each link opens the live chase screen in that state. The state switcher at the top of that page does the same. Add "-dry" to see the dry-run version, for example chase.html#partial-dry.
