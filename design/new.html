What the tool does

  1. Records the ask now as the cap.
  2. Places a post-only buy at the best bid.
  3. Moves it up with the best bid, at most every 5 s, never above the cap.
  4. After the timeout: cancels, confirms, re-reads the fill, then sends one IOC at the cap for the rest.
  5. Before the IOC: checks the rest against both Kraken minimums. A rest below a minimum stops as "not filled".
